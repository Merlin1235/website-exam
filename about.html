About Us

Greenfields Community Resource Centre is dedicated to improving the lives of community members by providing education, technology training, entrepreneurship support and community development programmes.

Our Objectives

  • Improve digital literacy within the community.
  • Provide entrepreneurship training and support.
  • Support youth development and mentorship.
  • Improve access to technology and information.
  • Promote community development.
